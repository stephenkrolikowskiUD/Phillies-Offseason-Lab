Inside the model

This is an illustrative 2027 hiring-demo sandbox. Player names are real; availability, contracts, statistics, ratings, baseline roster totals and the $280M budget are scenario assumptions, not verified forecasts. All data is embedded in this file.

Fit score: each slider weights a 0–100 scenario rating for plate discipline, outfield defense, or buy-low value (the gap between xwOBA and wOBA). Weights are normalized; setting all three to zero gives every target an equal score. Relievers have neutral offensive ratings and zero outfield-defense rating.

Team effects: payroll adds annual tax cost. Each hitter replaces a 450-PA, 29.4%-chase baseline slot in a 6,200-PA team. Each outfielder replaces 1,000 innings at −2 OAA. Each reliever replaces 60 innings at 4.12 xERA in a 580-IP bullpen. Up to 6 hitters, 3 outfielders and 8 relievers can be added to keep the allocation bounded.

No trades are executed. Prospect costs, outgoing salaries, tax penalties, roster eligibility and contract negotiation are outside this simplified model. The budget is a planning target, not the official CBT threshold. Changes save in this browser; export downloads a portable scenario summary.
